Comments and To Do's

Notes on the map: a comment is something worth knowing (blue flag); a To Do is work to do (orange flag).

  • Add one with the To Do and Comment buttons on a part's details, the note button on a scenario step, or the + on the Notes list.
  • A To Do has a priority: Must do (broken, risky or blocking), Should do (worth doing soon, the usual), or Nice to have. Choose it when you write the To Do, or change it on the To Do's panel. Lists put must-do first.
  • A note can have an Area: one of the stock questions (Security, Reliability, Data and the rest, or your library's own). It is No area unless you choose one in the editor. The Notes list shows the area, and the inspection report lists a specialist's notes under their area, beside the To Do's the agent left answering that stock question.
  • Every note says who wrote it: "you", a person's name, or "the agent, asked by" and the name of the person who asked, for notes the agent left while answering someone.
  • Click a flag on the map or a note in a list to open it: edit it, mark it done, or delete it. It opens over the map and the view stays where it is, zoom and all; Show on Map goes to where it is pinned, and closing it brings you back.
  • The To Do's and Comments checkboxes show or hide them, on the map and in the lists. A third, Agent comments, shows or hides the comments the agent left, so the list can show what people wrote and the To Do's alone, as the viewers do. It starts off once the system has more than twelve of the agent's comments, and on otherwise; once you tick or untick it, BigBrain remembers your choice for that system. Done To Do's sink to the bottom, dimmed.
  • A comment can be folded: kept, with its words, but out of the list and off the flags, behind a "3 folded" line at the end of the Notes list and of a part's notes. Click the line to see them. Fold or Unfold a comment from its right-click menu or with the button on the note (bigbrain notes --id <note> --fold or --unfold). The agent's check folds its own comments that only repeat the part's own description or an answer's takeaway (see below). Nothing is deleted, and the viewers put folded comments behind their "more" line too.
  • When the code a note rests on changes, the note above the sidebar counts it among what rests on code that changed. Update with Comments and To Do's ticked has your agent check each such note against just the code that changed under it, in one run. A note that still holds stops being flagged; one whose words are no longer right is reworded, with Restore Earlier Words to undo it; one that is no longer true is flagged with the agent's reason, for you to Delete Note or Keep It. The agent never deletes a note. Still True says you checked it yourself.
  • The same run weighs the agent's own comments it has not looked at yet, up to forty, reading no code: each beside the part's description and the takeaways of the answers on that part. One that says nothing those do not already say is folded, with the agent's reason on the note; one that adds a number, a condition or a workaround is kept as it is. The Update sheet ticks Comments and To Do's when a note rests on code that changed, or when more than twelve of the agent's comments wait to be weighed. Unfolding one the agent folded keeps its reason, so the next check does not fold it again.
