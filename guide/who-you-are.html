Who you are

Everything you make carries your name: comments, To Do's, questions, and your part in an inspection.

  • BigBrain takes your name and email from git. To use others, open BigBrain > Settings > You.
  • The person menu in the toolbar (it shows your first name) tells you who you are.
  • On a shared Mac, each person puts their own name in Settings > You before they start. (The demo tours show Sam and Priya taking part; in the demo the story's people are themselves.)
  • Signing in, for a seat, does not change the name and email your work carries. When you are signed in with another email, Settings > You says so and offers a button to use it; whether you do is up to you.
  • Work made with the Act as of earlier versions still says so, for example "Sam (impersonated by Maya)".
