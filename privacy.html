Summary

  • No cookies. This site does not set cookies of any kind.
  • No analytics or tracking. There are no analytics scripts, tags, pixels, or third-party trackers embedded in this site.
  • No forms, no accounts. This site has no sign-up forms, contact forms, or logins, and does not ask visitors for any personal information.

Information We Collect

bigbraincorp.com is a static informational website. We do not operate any server-side code, database, or analytics on this site, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from visitors browsing it.

This site is hosted on GitHub Pages. Like any web host, GitHub's infrastructure may automatically log basic technical information (such as IP address, browser type, and request timestamps) for security and abuse-prevention purposes. That logging is performed by GitHub, not by BigBrainCorp LLC. See GitHub's Privacy Statement for details.

Global Privacy Control (GPC) and Do Not Track

This site does not sell or share personal information, does not perform cross-context behavioral advertising, and does not use tracking cookies, so there are no opt-out choices to apply. As a result, honoring a Global Privacy Control or Do Not Track signal has no practical effect on how this site operates: no data collection or sharing takes place regardless of the signal. If that changes in the future, this policy will be updated and this site will honor GPC signals for any applicable opt-out rights.

Third-Party Links

This site links to third-party destinations, including ossscan.com, our product site, which has its own privacy policy. Once you leave bigbraincorp.com, this policy no longer applies, and the privacy practices of the destination site govern.

Children's Privacy

This site is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal data from anyone under 13 years of age.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time if the site's data practices change. The effective date above will be updated accordingly.
