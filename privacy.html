Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 7, 2026  ·  Site: bigbraincorp.com  ·  Contact: Jim@BigBrainCorp.com

Summary

  • No cookies. This site does not set cookies of any kind.
  • No analytics or tracking. There are no analytics scripts, tags, pixels, or third-party trackers embedded in this site.
  • No forms, no accounts. This site has no sign-up forms, contact forms, or logins. The one tool page described below asks for a name to put on your notes, and keeps it in your own browser; it is never sent to us.
  • The BigBrain Visualizer app has an account, and it is small. The license service behind the Mac app holds the email address your sign-in provider verified, a device hash for each Mac you use, your seat and license state, and a log of sign-ins and activations. It never sees a password, and nothing about your systems, code, maps or notes ever reaches it. The section below has the details.
  • Nothing is sold or shared. Because no personal data is collected, none is sold, shared, or used for advertising.

Information We Collect

bigbraincorp.com is a static informational website. We do not operate any server-side code, database, or analytics on this site, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from visitors browsing it.

This site is hosted on GitHub Pages. Like any web host, GitHub's infrastructure may automatically log basic technical information (such as IP address, browser type, and request timestamps) for security and abuse-prevention purposes. That logging is performed by GitHub, not by BigBrainCorp LLC. See GitHub's Privacy Statement for details.

The File Viewer Page

This site hosts one tool page, a viewer for files we share directly with beta testers. The viewer runs entirely in your browser. The files you open with it are read, unlocked, and shown on your own computer; they are never uploaded to this site, to us, or to anyone else. Passwords you type are kept only until you close the browser window.

When you add your first note, the viewer asks for a name and, if you like, an email address to put on your notes. These, and settings such as the voice and the layout, are kept in your browser's storage for this site, not in cookies, and are never sent to us. Your notes go into the file you save, and reach us only if you choose to send that file back. You can remove what the viewer keeps by clearing this site's data in your browser.

The viewer can read steps aloud using the voices your browser offers. Voices marked "online" are provided over the internet by your browser's maker, so the words being read are sent to that provider; voices without that mark run on your computer.

The BigBrain Visualizer App and Its License Service

BigBrain System Visualizer, BigBrain Visualizer for short, is a Mac app. Its demos, its viewers, and opening a file someone sent you need no account and send nothing to us. Building a system of your own needs a seat, and a seat needs an account with our license service. This section says what that service holds and what it never holds.

What the license service holds

  • Your email address, as verified by the sign-in provider you chose. It is your account: it names you on your seat, and it is where we send notices about your account.
  • A device hash for each Mac you have used the app on while signed in. It is a one-way hash made on the Mac from stable hardware identifiers; it cannot be turned back into those identifiers and cannot find your Mac on a network. It exists so a seat can be on one Mac at a time. A name you give the Mac in the app is kept beside it so you can tell your Macs apart.
  • Your seat and license state: whether your seat is on, what turns it on (a beta license, or later a subscription), when it ends, and which Mac it is on now. For a team, which email addresses hold the team's seats.
  • Audit events: one record for each sign-in, activation, refresh refusal, beta license, free access grant and payment event, with the time, the account and the Mac involved. They let us answer a question about your account and spot a seat being moved in a way a seat should not move.
  • A session: when you sign in, the service gives the app a session token. The service keeps only a hash of that token, never the token itself.

Signing in

You sign in with Apple, Google or Microsoft. The sign-in happens at a hosted identity service, Microsoft Entra External ID, which talks to the provider you chose and hands the app a signed token naming your verified email address. Our service checks that token and nothing else. We never see, receive or store a password, and we hold no credentials for any of those providers. Each provider's own privacy policy covers what it does when you sign in with it.

What never reaches the service

Nothing about your systems. Your code stays in your checkouts, the map, the scenarios, the comments, To Do's and notes stay in a folder on your Mac, and files you send to a specialist go from you to them. The service has no field for any of it: every request the app can make is a short message about your account, your seat or your Mac, and anything else is refused. The app sends us no telemetry or analytics.

Your own coding agent, Claude Code or GitHub Copilot, reads your code to build the map and answer questions about it. That tool runs on your Mac under your own account with its provider, and sends what it reads to that provider under the provider's terms and privacy policy, not ours. We do not control that tool and receive nothing from it. The demos and sent files need no coding agent.

Downloads and updates

The app is downloaded from, and updates itself from, storage we run on Microsoft Azure. Like any web host, that storage logs basic technical information about each download, such as the IP address and the time, which we use to count downloads and nothing else. During the closed beta the download listing asks for a beta code; the code is checked and not stored with anything about you.

Payments, later

Nothing is for sale during the beta. When payments open, Stripe will handle them: you will pay on Stripe's hosted checkout page from inside the app and manage the subscription on Stripe's customer portal. Your card details go to Stripe and never touch us; we receive from Stripe what turns your seat on: a customer reference, the plan and the subscription's status. Stripe's own privacy policy covers what it holds.

Who processes the data

We do not sell, rent or trade personal data. The service runs on Microsoft Azure, sign-in runs through Microsoft Entra External ID, and payments will run through Stripe. Those are the only processors. We may disclose data if the law requires it or to protect the rights, property or safety of BigBrainCorp LLC, our customers or the public, and we will tell you where the law lets us.

For people in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland: the account, device and seat records are processed to perform the contract that gives you your seat, and the audit events are processed in our legitimate interest in keeping seats to the people who hold them.

How long records are kept

Your account, device and seat records are kept for as long as your account is active and for a reasonable period afterwards for accounting and fraud prevention. A Mac you remove from your account in the app is kept only as history of the seat.

Audit events are kept for a fixed period and then deleted. The period is to be confirmed and will be stated here before the beta ends.

Your rights and how to ask for deletion

You may ask to see, correct or delete the personal data we hold about you. Write to Info@BigBrainCorp.com from the email address you sign in with, and we will answer within 30 days. Deleting your account ends your seat and removes your email address, your devices and your seat records; audit events are kept until their period runs out and are then deleted with the rest. Deletion changes nothing on your Mac: your maps and notes are yours and stay where they are.

Global Privacy Control (GPC) and Do Not Track

This site does not sell or share personal information, does not perform cross-context behavioral advertising, and does not use tracking cookies, so there are no opt-out choices to apply. As a result, honoring a Global Privacy Control or Do Not Track signal has no practical effect on how this site operates: no data collection or sharing takes place regardless of the signal. If that changes in the future, this policy will be updated and this site will honor GPC signals for any applicable opt-out rights.
